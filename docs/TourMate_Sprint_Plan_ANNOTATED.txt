# 🗓️ TourMate AI — Final Agile Sprint Planning

Board Type: Scrum | Sprint Duration: 1 Week | Total Sprints: 7
Team: 2 Frontend · 2 Backend · 2 AI

> Each sprint was conducted over a one-week period, followed by a sprint review and retrospective meeting to evaluate progress and refine the backlog. QA activities were embedded within each sprint to ensure iterative validation and continuous quality assurance.

---

> 🔄 **Model-alignment update (2026-06-16):** Tasks affected by the corrected class diagram are annotated inline with `↳ [MODEL UPDATE 2026-06-16]`. Change clusters applied: (1) **ItineraryStop** join entity (Day → ItineraryStop → Place); (2) 3-tier **Place** taxonomy + AI fields (tags, placeProfile, embedding, popularityScore) and subtypes (Attraction/Hotel/Restaurant); (3) **Location**/**OpeningHours** 1:1 + `timezone`; (4) **Booking → Reservation → Payment → Receipt** subsystem (Reservation → placeId only). Related model changes also noted: TravelerProfile `dimensionScores` (6-axis TravelDimension), Conversation/Message, SavedPlace, Recommendation, Feedback. No tasks were added, removed, or renumbered.
